Let's add one half and one third. The whole bars are the same size, but the pieces are different sizes. First, make equal pieces. Six is a common multiple of two and three. One half equals three sixths. One third equals two sixths. Now add three sixths and two sixths. Three plus two is five. The pieces are still sixths, so the denominator stays six. One half plus one third equals five sixths. We changed the size of the pieces, not the amount shaded. Try adding one half and one sixth yourself.
