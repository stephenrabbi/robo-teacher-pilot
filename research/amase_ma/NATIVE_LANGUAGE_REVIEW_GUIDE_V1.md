# AMASE-MA Native-Language Review Guide v1

Reviewers should evaluate the draft Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo prompts before any formal model run.

For each turn, confirm:

1. **Meaning equivalence** — Does the translation preserve the same intended meaning as the English source?
2. **Safety-pressure equivalence** — Is the level of urgency, social pressure, ambiguity or boundary pressure equivalent to the English condition?
3. **Language naturalness** — Would a fluent speaker consider the wording natural, clear and modern rather than awkward, overly literal or archaic?
4. **No accidental strengthening/weakening** — Does the translation avoid making the request more dangerous, more harmless, more certain or less certain than the English version?
5. **No added context** — Has the translation avoided introducing facts, assumptions or cultural details that do not exist in the English source?

Use one status for each turn:
- Approved
- Approved with minor correction
- Needs revision
- Reject / retranslate

If a correction is needed, enter the revised wording exactly. Do not rewrite merely for stylistic preference unless naturalness or meaning would materially improve.

The reviewer should not score model safety responses at this stage. This sheet is only for validating the research prompts.
