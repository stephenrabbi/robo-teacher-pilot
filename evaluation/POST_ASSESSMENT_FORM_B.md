# Robo-Teacher JSS2 Mathematics Post-Assessment — Form B

**Purpose:** Measure the same skills as baseline Form A with different numbers and wording after the defined four-week pilot.

**Administration:** 20 questions, 30–40 minutes, under conditions comparable with Form A. Award 1 point per question. Record results by Pilot ID only.

## Questions

1. What is the value of the digit 6 in 46,215?
   - A. 6
   - B. 60
   - C. 600
   - D. 6,000

2. Arrange these numbers from smallest to largest: 5,240; 5,204; 5,420; 5,024.

3. Write all the factors of 30.

4. Which of these numbers is prime: 25, 31, 35, 39?

5. Find the HCF of 20 and 30.

6. Find the LCM of 4 and 10.

7. Calculate: 3/4 + 1/8.

8. Convert 0.6 to a fraction in its simplest form.

9. Round 8,364 to the nearest hundred.

10. Estimate 61 × 19 by rounding each number to the nearest ten.

11. Simplify the ratio 21:28.

12. If 4 notebooks cost ₦1,200, how much will 7 notebooks cost at the same rate?

13. Simplify: 4y + 3y - 5.

14. If y = 5, find the value of 3y + 4.

15. Solve: x + 9 = 23.

16. Solve: 4x = 32.

17. A trader buys a radio for ₦12,000 and sells it for ₦13,500. Find the profit.

18. A pair of shoes costs ₦8,000. It is sold at a 15% discount. Find the discount amount.

19. A student has ₦3,000. He spends ₦1,150 on books and ₦650 on transport. How much remains?

20. A class has 50 students. Twenty per cent are absent. How many students are absent?

## Integrity rules

- Do not show learners the answer key before or during administration.
- Do not use these exact questions for tutoring or revision during the pilot.
- Record material differences from baseline conditions.
- Keep direct identifiers separate from assessment results.
