# Robo-Teacher Student Feedback Survey

**Purpose:** Capture student perceptions after using Robo-Teacher during the pilot.

Use a 1–5 scale for each statement:

1 = Strongly disagree  
2 = Disagree  
3 = Neither agree nor disagree  
4 = Agree  
5 = Strongly agree

## Statements

1. Robo-Teacher was easy for me to access when I needed Mathematics help.
2. Robo-Teacher's explanations helped me understand Mathematics better.
3. Robo-Teacher showed enough steps for me to follow how an answer was reached.
4. Robo-Teacher made me more comfortable asking questions I might not ask in class.
5. Robo-Teacher helped me work through a problem instead of only giving me an answer.
6. I would use Robo-Teacher again when I need help with Mathematics.
7. I would recommend Robo-Teacher to another student who needs Mathematics help.

## Optional open questions

8. What was the most helpful thing about Robo-Teacher?

9. What should Robo-Teacher improve?

10. Describe one Mathematics topic that Robo-Teacher helped you understand better.

## Data capture

Record responses using Pilot ID rather than student name. Keep the feedback dataset separate from contact identifiers.

Suggested summary metrics:

- Average score for each statement
- Overall mean score across statements 1–7
- Percentage selecting 4 or 5 for each statement
- Common themes from questions 8–10

Do not report individual student responses publicly without appropriate consent. Use aggregate or anonymized findings in the competition application.
