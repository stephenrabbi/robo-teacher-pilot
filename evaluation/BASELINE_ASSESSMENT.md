# Robo-Teacher JSS2 Mathematics Baseline Assessment

**Purpose:** Establish a pre-pilot learning baseline for students participating in the Robo-Teacher evaluation.

**Recommended administration:** 20 questions, 30–40 minutes, before a defined evaluation period. Use the same assessment for all participants under the same conditions.

**Scoring:** 1 point per question; maximum 20 points. Record only the Pilot ID with the score in the evaluation dataset. Keep names/contact identifiers in the private roster only.

> **Teacher review before administration:** This assessment is aligned to the current topic scope documented in `tutor.py`, but the teacher/research lead should confirm that every item matches the school's actual JSS2 scheme of work before use.

## Questions

### Whole numbers and place value
1. What is the value of the digit 7 in 57,324?
   - A. 7
   - B. 70
   - C. 700
   - D. 7,000

2. Arrange these numbers from smallest to largest: 4,305; 4,350; 4,053; 4,530.

### Factors, multiples and prime numbers
3. Write all the factors of 24.

4. Which of these numbers is prime: 21, 27, 29, 33?

### LCM and HCF
5. Find the HCF of 18 and 24.

6. Find the LCM of 6 and 8.

### Fractions and decimals
7. Calculate: 2/3 + 1/6.

8. Convert 0.75 to a fraction in its simplest form.

### Approximation and estimation
9. Round 6,748 to the nearest hundred.

10. Estimate 49 × 21 by rounding each number to the nearest ten.

### Ratio, proportion and rate
11. Simplify the ratio 18:24.

12. If 3 exercise books cost ₦900, how much will 5 exercise books cost at the same rate?

### Algebraic expressions
13. Simplify: 3x + 5x - 2.

14. If x = 4, find the value of 2x + 7.

### Simple linear equations
15. Solve: x + 7 = 19.

16. Solve: 3x = 21.

### Profit, loss and percentages
17. A trader buys a bag for ₦8,000 and sells it for ₦9,000. Find the profit.

18. A shirt costs ₦5,000. It is sold at a 10% discount. Find the discount amount.

### Mixed application
19. A student has ₦2,000. She spends ₦750 on books and ₦450 on food. How much money is left?

20. A class has 40 students. 25% of them are absent. How many students are absent?
