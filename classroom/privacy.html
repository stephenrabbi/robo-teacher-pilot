Robo-Teacher is designed for learners, including minors. We use data minimisation, pseudonymous learner identifiers and permission-based use. We do not sell personal data and we do not currently use advertising or behavioural analytics cookies.

1. Who controls your data

Earlyon-Tech Brainery operates Robo-Teacher and is responsible for deciding why and how personal data is processed for the service.

2. Data we may process

  • Browser classroom: nickname, class level, learner code, pseudonymous learner/session identifiers, learning questions and AI responses, practice/diagnostic results, progress and recommendations.
  • Optional learning media: images, camera captures, voice recordings or whiteboard drawings you deliberately submit for tutoring. These are processed to answer the learning request.
  • Messaging pilot: where Telegram or WhatsApp pilot channels are used, the platform identifier needed to recognise an approved learner, school, pilot ID, messages and submitted learning media may be processed.
  • Device-local information: language and voice preferences, saved lessons, local learning continuity, local teacher roster names and QA/device-timing information may be stored in your browser. Local teacher roster names are intended to remain on that device.
  • Technical/security information: limited service and security events necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

We ask learners to use a nickname and not to put full names, phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses into learning questions. Interaction-log excerpts are limited and common direct identifiers are redacted before storage where technically detected.

3. Why we process data

  • Provide tutoring, practice, diagnostics, progress continuity and teacher-support features.
  • Authenticate learner codes and protect the closed pilot.
  • Maintain safety, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.
  • Evaluate and improve educational performance using proportionate, pseudonymous pilot evidence.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Children and consent

Where a learner is a minor, Robo-Teacher should be used with permission from a parent/guardian or the participating school/teacher acting within its lawful authority. The classroom asks users to confirm this permission before starting. Where consent is the required legal basis, it may be withdrawn, although processing already lawfully performed is not automatically invalidated.

5. Legal bases

Depending on the activity, processing may rely on consent, performance of a requested service, legitimate interests in operating a safe educational service, or compliance with law. Consent is used where legally required, including optional third-party cookies/content and processing involving minors where applicable under Nigerian data-protection rules.

6. Who may receive data

We use service providers only where needed to operate Robo-Teacher. These currently include Render for hosting, Google Gemini for AI tutoring and multimodal/voice processing, Google Sheets for private pilot records, Telegram for Telegram interactions, Twilio for the WhatsApp integration/migration path, and PhET for optional interactive simulations. A third-party simulation is blocked until you choose to allow external content.

7. International processing

Some technology providers may process data outside Nigeria. We seek to use appropriate contractual, technical and legal safeguards for such processing and limit what is sent to what the feature needs.

8. Retention

We keep data only for as long as it is needed for the active learning service, pilot administration, evaluation, security or legal obligations, after which it should be deleted or de-identified. Browser-local data remains on the device until it is cleared, replaced or removed by the user/browser. The Privacy choices panel includes a “Clear this device data” control.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Automated learning recommendations

Robo-Teacher uses automated processing to recommend learning activities and difficulty. These recommendations are educational support and are not intended to make legal, employment, credit or other decisions producing significant legal or socio-economic effects.

11. Security

We use pseudonymous identifiers, access controls, private service credentials, signed classroom sessions and data minimisation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so learners should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data-rights requests, email oluwafemiherbert@gmail.com with enough information for us to understand and verify the request without sending unnecessary sensitive data.

Robo-Teacher is an educational technology product of Earlyon-Tech Brainery.
