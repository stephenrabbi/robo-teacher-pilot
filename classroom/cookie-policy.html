Do we need cookie consent? Yes for optional cookies or similar third-party tracking/storage. Robo-Teacher therefore blocks optional external simulation content until you choose to allow it. The classroom does not currently run advertising or behavioural analytics cookies.

1. What we use

Robo-Teacher currently uses browser local/session storage for essential or user-requested functionality such as language/voice preferences, session continuity, saved lessons, pseudonymous learner continuity, local teacher roster names and QA/device-timing information.

2. Analytics

We did not identify a dedicated advertising, Google Analytics, behavioural analytics or cross-site tracking script in the current v2.6 classroom. Device load timings shown in Teacher QA are designed to stay in the browser.

3. Optional third-party content

Interactive simulations may come from PhET or another disclosed third party. Loading an external iframe can disclose technical information such as IP address and browser information to that provider and may allow it to use cookies or similar storage. Robo-Teacher asks before loading optional external content.

4. Your choices

Choose “Essential only” to keep optional external content blocked, or “Allow external content” to permit it. You can reopen Privacy choices from the classroom footer. The choice is saved on your device so the app can remember it.

5. Clearing storage

You can use “Clear this device data” in Privacy choices or your browser settings. Clearing storage may remove saved lessons, local preferences and continuity information on that device.

6. Changes

If we add non-essential analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies later, we will update this policy and obtain the consent required before enabling them.
