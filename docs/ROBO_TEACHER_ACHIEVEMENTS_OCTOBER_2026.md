# Robo-Teacher — Achievements and Evidence Profile
**Status: October 2026 | Earlyon-Tech Brainery | Lagos State, Nigeria**

## What we have built
Robo-Teacher is a teacher-founded AI learning product developed through Earlyon-Tech Brainery to make guided instruction and practice more accessible to learners. Founder Herbert Oluwafemi Stephen built the initiative from direct experience teaching large secondary-school classes, including a mathematics class of 74 learners. It started through WhatsApp and Telegram and now has a deployed web-based AI classroom.

The current mathematics classroom serves JSS1–JSS3 as the initial curriculum scope, with the longer-term product vision extending across secondary-school subjects and levels. The deployed product includes guided explanations, learner questions, practice and diagnostic activities, progress features and multimodal teaching interfaces. Text, voice, photo/camera and whiteboard workflows have been developed and tested. English, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa are available as language selections. These statements describe product scope and functionality, not proof that every feature is perfect on every device.

## Demonstrated accomplishments
- **A working, deployed learning application:** Production web classroom running on Render, with repeated GitHub-based improvements and automated regression checks.
- **A learner-centred interface:** Authentic classroom photograph and a preserved speaking founder avatar, step-by-step onboarding, privacy/permission controls, and responsive welcome-page layout.
- **Real learner participation:** An August 2026 two-school mathematics evaluation of **56 matched learners**, from Ise Junior High School (25) and Tio College (31).
- **Observed learning progress:** Mean mathematics assessment scores increased **12.7% to 26.5%**, a gain of **13.8 percentage points**; **51 of 56** learners (91.1%) improved. Mean feedback was **4.84/5** across 56 responses.
- **Documented learning engagement:** The private source sheet's 11–12 October aggregate snapshot showed **213 logged messaging interactions**—121 WhatsApp and 92 Telegram—from the evaluation cohort's continuing use. This is a later cumulative snapshot than the earlier 188-interaction GitHub record.
- **Web-product user testing:** **29 distinct learners** tested the V2.5 classroom on 4 September 2026: 21 Android, five Windows and three iPhone device records. Their feedback supplied practical direction for subsequent development.
- **Continuing school implementation:** **331 students have been reported onboarded** across school activity. This is a separately reported reach milestone; the deduplicated dated register remains to be reconciled and must not be confused with 331 measured learning outcomes.
- **Latest device acceptance:** Founder-confirmed seven-of-seven Android checks on 12 October 2026 covered welcome-page fit, images/avatar, guide, founder audio start/stop, registration/permission, classroom handover, and keyboard/camera/microphone.
- **Verified release state:** Production commit `567456a678b9fcc51d7c5c29cf904e0737be99a7` (PR #99) deployed live on Render; accompanying automated checks passed.

## Understanding the product's development
The 4 September exercise recorded usability issues in the then-current V2.5 build. Those historical observations should be used to explain the iterative development journey rather than described as faults proven to persist in October. The October Android acceptance confirms performance on the tested Android device; it does not by itself constitute another 29-person test or current iPhone acceptance.

## Outcomes: sound interpretation
The 56-learner evaluation provides **descriptive matched pre/post evidence of progress**, not a randomized causal impact estimate. Findings are meaningful as early field evidence while leaving room for more rigorous follow-up. No paying customers, retention results, cross-device persistence claims or learning efficacy beyond observed assessment changes should be implied without their own evidence.
