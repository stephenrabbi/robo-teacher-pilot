## Conditions to resolve before any real learner identity rollout

- Confirm data minimization, approved retention/deletion/export, vendors and information-security contact.
- Approve a safe shared-phone design, token revocation and age-appropriate accessibility.
- Test login, enrollment, abuse controls and recovery against impersonation and cross-learner access.
- Obtain independent privacy/security and safeguarding review, with a recorded go/no-go decision.
- Introduce production functionality only through a *new separately reviewed release PR*; keep prototype PR #85 unmerged.
